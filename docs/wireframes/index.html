Notes
  1. Each action asks for an optional reason and writes it to the Activity log.
  2. "Suspend user…" offers 1 day, 7 days or until lifted. A suspended user can still read but can't post.
  3. Users (admin only): search, see their role, suspend, and change role.
  4. Featured row: pick recipes and reorder them with ↑↓.
  5. Dictionary: an editable table with Malayalam, English and Manglish aliases, plus a "used in N recipes" count.
  6. On phone: the sidebar becomes a top tab list and the cards stack.
