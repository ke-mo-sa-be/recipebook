Kerala Fish Curry
മീൻ കറി

Aby Ammini · Ernakulam

TraditionalMetric−4 people+
Seer fish½ kg
Coconut milk1 glass
Shallots10
Chilli1 tbsp
Ginger1 inch
Garlic6 cloves
Curry leaves2 sprigs
Saltto taste

Steps

Start cooking
1

Soak kudampuli in a little warm water for 10 minutes.

10 min
2

Crush shallots, ginger and garlic. Sauté in coconut oil until golden and fragrant.

3

Add chilli and turmeric powder on low heat. Stir for a minute so it doesn't burn.

1 min
4

Add the kudampuli with its water, salt and a glass of water. Bring to a boil.

5

Slide in the fish pieces. Cover and cook on medium heat.

12 min

Tastes even better the next day. Make it in the morning for dinner.

Desktop rules
  • No bottom nav on desktop. The top bar has the brand, the search composer, Home and Saved, the black Add recipe button and your avatar.
  • Content max width is 1200 px. Card grids run 4 columns on Home and 3 on the cookbook. The recipe page splits into ingredients on the left and steps on the right, with Start cooking always in view.
  • Cooking mode on desktop or tablet: the step on the left, the ingredient checklist with stickers on the right.
